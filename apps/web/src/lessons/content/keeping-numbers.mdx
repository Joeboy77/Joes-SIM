The accumulator holds only one number. To work with two, you need somewhere to keep the first
while you read the second. That is what mailboxes are for.

- `STA name` stores the accumulator in a mailbox. The accumulator keeps its value.
- `LDA name` loads a mailbox into the accumulator. The mailbox keeps its value.
- `name DAT 5` reserves a mailbox called `name` that starts as 5. `DAT` on its own starts at 0.

`DAT` lines are data, not instructions, so put them **after** the `HLT`. Otherwise the Little
Man would try to run them.

## Reading a mailbox nobody filled

If you `LDA` from a mailbox that was never given a value, you get 0. The simulator points this
out, because it is usually a mistake: a missing `STA`, or a `DAT` without its starting value.
