# Sharing and backups

Joe's SIM has no accounts. Your work stays in your browser, and you choose what to share.

## Share a program

In the simulator, press **Share**. The link holds the program, its inputs, the dialect and the
view, compressed into the address. Anyone who opens it sees exactly what you saw. Nothing is
uploaded or stored on a server. Messaging apps show a preview card with the program's first
lines.

## Program files

In the simulator, the 📄 button opens your files. **Export this program** saves it as an `.lmc`
file, and **Import .lmc** opens one. `.lmc` files are plain text, so other LMC tools can read
them too.

## Move everything to another device

On the [Progress](/progress) page, choose **Download a backup**. It holds all your files, your
progress and your teacher packs. On the other device, open the same page and choose **Restore
from a backup**. This replaces what is there, so it asks first.
