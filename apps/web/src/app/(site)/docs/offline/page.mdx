# Offline and installing

After your first visit, Joe's SIM works with no connection at all: the simulator, lessons,
challenges, teacher packs and your progress. Everything runs in your browser.

## Install it as an app

- **On a phone:** open the browser's menu and choose **Add to home screen** or **Install app**.
- **On a computer:** look for the install icon at the end of the address bar, or choose
  **Install Joe's SIM** from the browser's menu.

The app opens in its own window and works offline.

## Updates

When a new version is ready, Joe's SIM shows a message. Choose **Reload** to start using it.
Your work and progress are kept.
