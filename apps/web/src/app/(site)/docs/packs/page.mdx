# Teacher packs

A teacher pack is a list of lessons, challenges and starter programs in the order your course
needs, with a dialect. It is one small file, so you can share it however you like: in your
course page, by email, or as a link.

## Make a pack

1. Go to [Teacher packs](/packs) and choose **Make a new pack**, or open the DCIT 207 pack and
   choose **Make my own copy to edit**.
2. Give it a title, and add lessons, challenges, headings and programs. Move them into order.
3. Choose **Save the pack**. It is saved in your browser.

## Share it

From the pack's page, choose **Download the pack file** or **Copy a link to this pack**. The
link holds the whole pack, so nothing is uploaded. Long packs make long links; the file always
works.

## How learners use it

Learners open the link, or open the file from [Teacher packs](/packs). They see the items in
order, and ticks for the lessons and challenges they have finished. Their progress stays on
their own device, so nobody, including you, can see it. Learners can download a backup from
the [Progress](/progress) page if you want to see their work.
