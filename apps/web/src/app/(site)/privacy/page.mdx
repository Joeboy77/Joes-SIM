# Privacy

**Joe's SIM collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no cookies for tracking, no
analytics and no advertising.

## What stays on your device

Your programs, your progress, your predictions and your teacher packs are saved in your
browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
Clearing your browser's site data deletes them, so download a backup from the
[Progress](/progress) page if you want to keep them.

## Share links

When you share a program, the program is inside the link itself. It is not uploaded. When a
messaging app shows a preview card, it asks this site to draw a picture of the program from the
link; the site draws it and keeps nothing.

## Leaderboards, the gallery and short links

These are optional, and only exist if the site you are using has switched them on. Nothing is
sent unless you choose to use them:

- **Posting a score** sends your program and a nickname. The server checks that the program passes
  the tests, keeps your nickname and your scores, and throws the program away.
- **Publishing to the gallery** stores the program, its title, its description and your nickname.
  Everyone can see them until you delete the program.
- **A short link** stores the share link it points to, so anyone with the short link can open the
  program.

The first time you use one of these, your browser makes a random key and keeps it on your device.
The server stores a scrambled form of it (a salted hash) next to your entries. This lets this
browser change or delete them, with no account. To stop abuse, the server counts requests per
visitor using a scrambled form of your IP address; the address itself is not stored. Choose a
nickname that is not your real name.

You can take your scores off a leaderboard, or delete a program you published, at any time from
the same browser.

## The site itself

Like every website, the server that sends you the pages sees your IP address and which pages
you asked for, as part of delivering them. Joe's SIM itself records nothing about visits; the
hosting service may keep its standard server logs for a short time, for security.
