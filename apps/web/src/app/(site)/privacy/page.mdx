# Privacy

**Joe's SIM collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no cookies for tracking, no
analytics and no advertising.

## What stays on your device

Your programs, your progress, your predictions and your teacher packs are saved in your
browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
Clearing your browser's site data deletes them, so download a backup from the
[Progress](/progress) page if you want to keep them.

## Share links

When you share a program, the program is inside the link itself. It is not uploaded. When a
messaging app shows a preview card, it asks this site to draw a picture of the program from the
link; the site draws it and keeps nothing.

## The site itself

Like every website, the server that sends you the pages sees your IP address and which pages
you asked for, as part of delivering them. Joe's SIM itself records nothing about visits; the
hosting service may keep its standard server logs for a short time, for security.
